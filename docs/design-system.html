01 · Colour

Colour & contrast

Gold is a dark-background colour. On Warm Cream it decorates; it never carries text. Muted text on cream uses --colour-text-muted, not Stone Grey.

  • Brass Gold --colour-gold #B8922A

    Brand. Rules, marks, CTAs. Text on dark only.

  • Dark Gold --colour-gold-dark #7A5F1A

    The only gold that may carry text on cream.

  • Champagne --colour-gold-light #C9A84C

    Hover on gold buttons; highlights on dark.

  • Deep Black --colour-black #111111

    Anchor. Dark sections, body text.

  • Surface Dark --colour-surface-dark #1C1A18

    Dark section variant (IFL, CTA).

  • Warm Cream --colour-bg #F5F0E8

    Page ground.

  • Surface --colour-surface #EDE7DA

    Alternating sections, tiles.

  • Border --colour-border #D8D0C0

    Hairlines, input outlines.

  • Stone Grey --colour-stone #9E9790

    Decoration on light. Muted text on dark only.

  • Text Secondary --colour-text-sec #5C5650

    Supporting copy on light.

  • Text Muted --colour-text-muted #6E675E

    Labels, hints on light (new in v2.1).

10 · Media

Media & captions

.frame sets an aspect ratio and crops with object-fit: cover; ratios: --3-2, --4-3, --3-4, --21-9, --wide, --panel. Every photograph uses <picture> with WebP + JPEG, explicit width and height, and the manifest alt text. .figure__caption overlays a tag and a line on a gradient; .caption sits under the picture.

12 · Hero animation

Hero animation

13 · Rules

Rules

  • No literal hex colours outside tokens.css. Components reference tokens only.
  • Gold is punctuation, not paint: a rule, a numeral, a hairline, the CTA. Never body text on cream.
  • Monospace only for figures that describe physical reality or process state.
  • Every photograph: <picture>, WebP + JPEG, width and height, manifest alt text. Only the hero image is eager.
  • Content never depends on script. .reveal and .ifl--reveal apply only under html.js with motion allowed.
  • Total JavaScript under 15 KB unminified. Today: main.js 2.8 KB + contact.js 12.0 KB.
  • Australian English in copy, comments and filenames.
